What is RTP?

RTP (return to player) is the theoretical percentage of total wagers a game returns to players over a very large number of plays. It is a long-term statistical measure, not a prediction of the next result, a guaranteed return, or a signal that a game is due to pay out.

How are YonoVIP RTP figures updated?

RTP figures are intended to be updated from a connected data source. Until their source is verified, do not treat the displayed percentages as live or rely on them when wagering. RTP describes long-term averages, not individual results. Bet cautiously and only with money you can afford to lose.
